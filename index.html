The "Level-Down" Differentiator

"I am teaching a 10th-grade Biology lesson on DNA replication. I have three students who read at a 5th-grade level. Rewrite this complex paragraph [Paste Text] for a 5th-grade reading level while keeping all scientific vocabulary (but providing parenthetical definitions). Create 3 comprehension questions at the end."

AI-Resistant Assignment Generator

"Review my current essay prompt: [Paste Prompt]. Now, suggest 3 modifications that make this assignment harder for AI to complete without personal student input. Incorporate requirements for: local context, recent current events (past 2 weeks), and a mandatory physical artifact or classroom observation component."

Classroom Ready: Student Activities

Bring AI into the learning experience ethically and critically.

The Socratic Opponent

Students use a chatbot to debate a specific historical figure. The AI plays the figure, and students must use primary source evidence to challenge the AI's "memory."

Learning Outcome

"Evaluate the accuracy of AI-generated historical personas."

The AI Error Hunt

Ask an AI to explain a complex math problem or scientific theory. Give the output to students and challenge them to find 3 "hallucinations" or logical errors in its reasoning.

Learning Outcome

"Develop critical thinking and fact-checking skills."
